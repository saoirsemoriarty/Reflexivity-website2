Who We Are

Our mission & values

Reflexivity exists to make evaluation faster, more rigorous, and more useful for the people who commission it. We believe public services deserve evidence that arrives in time to shape decisions — not after them.

Transparent

We are open about what AI does and doesn't do in our work. Every method, model, and decision is documented and auditable. No black boxes.

Thoughtful

We don't use AI for the sake of it. Every tool we deploy is chosen because it genuinely improves the quality or speed of the evaluation — not because it's fashionable.

Compliant

We take data governance seriously. From GDPR to sector-specific regulations in health and social care, compliance is built into our process from the start — not bolted on at the end.

Our Approach

Transparent, considered, and quality-assured throughout.

We are not a software platform, and we are not another technology company that underestimates the messiness of real-world policy. We understand the scepticism around AI and we share it.

Our model is built on human expertise first, with AI where it genuinely adds speed and rigour — and not where it doesn't. We understand the regulatory landscape around data in health and social care, and we take it seriously. Sensitive data can be processed locally on our own infrastructure — nothing leaves your premises unless you want it to. Where cloud processing is appropriate, we use secure, GDPR-compliant servers with full audit trails.

Bespoke, Not Templated

We work alongside clients to design evaluations that fit their context. Where it adds value, we build and deploy custom models that run on your own hardware — no data leaving your laptop. We don't default to off-the-shelf AI when it isn't the right tool.

AI-Accelerated Analysis

AI supports data processing, pattern recognition, and rapid evidence synthesis — preserving human judgement for interviews, interpretation, and stakeholder engagement.

Compliant by Design

GDPR, health data regulations, and sector-specific requirements are built into our workflows from day one. Sensitive data stays on your infrastructure when needed, with full audit trails throughout.

Real-Time Insights

Faster evidence that keeps pace with policy decision-making, so findings arrive when they're still needed.
