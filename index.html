Who we are

We are an agency specialising in evaluations of complex systems across public health, health services and public policy. As an interdisciplinary team, combining methodological expertise, technical expertise and domain knowledge, we deliver rapid, rigorous insights that keep pace with decision making.

We use technology, including AI, in a transparent and considered way, applying it only where it strengthens our evaluations, while keeping strong audit trails and human oversight throughout.

We believe speed should buy time for what matters. By using technology to quicken the parts of evaluation it can handle, we free up more time for genuine engagement with people and participatory methods.

Areas of expertise

We take a health equity lens to all of our work, asking who benefits and who is left out.

What we evaluate

Health services and systems

From a single service to a whole pathway or system: how it works in practice, where it falls short, and what would make it better.

Public health programmes and policy

Prevention, screening and population health initiatives, from local pilot to national rollout.

Place-based programmes

Interventions rooted in a particular neighbourhood, town or local system, where the setting is part of the intervention.

How we evaluate

Realist and theory-based evaluation

For complex interventions with many moving parts: what works, for whom, in what circumstances, and why.

Qualitative and mixed-methods evaluation

Interviews, observation and documents, analysed rigorously and combined with the numbers so that each explains the other.

Quantitative and epidemiological analysis

Routine and survey data showing how health and service use vary by income, ethnicity, place, and other social conditions.

Participatory evaluation

Working with the people who use and deliver a service to shape the questions, gather the evidence, and make sense of the findings.
